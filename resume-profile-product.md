# Resume Profile - Product Engineering

## Senior Software Engineer

Senior Software Engineer with 30+ years of experience building and scaling software products that deliver measurable business value. Strong at turning product concepts into production systems, from schema and backend services to front-end implementation. Known for shipping practical features that improve customer experience and open new revenue opportunities.

## Skills Focus

- Product-focused full-stack delivery
- Feature design and iterative implementation
- Blazor and backend service development
- Commerce and subscription workflows
- API and third-party integration patterns
- SQL Server, Azure SQL, data modeling
- Reliability and maintainability improvements
- Stakeholder collaboration and execution
