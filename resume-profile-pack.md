# Resume Profile Pack

Use this file to quickly choose a targeted profile based on the role you are applying for.

## When To Use Each Profile (30-Second Checklist)

- Use **Enterprise Modernization** when the posting emphasizes legacy migration, refactoring, modernization, reliability, or business continuity.
- Use **Product Engineering** when the posting emphasizes customer-facing features, product delivery speed, subscriptions/commerce, or feature ownership.
- Use **Platform Architecture** when the posting emphasizes platform design, reusable services, long-term maintainability, or technical foundations.
- If two categories seem close, choose the one that best matches the top 3 responsibilities listed in the job description.
- After choosing a profile, mirror 8-12 keywords from the posting in your summary, skills, and top experience bullets.
